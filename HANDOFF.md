## Accepted decisions and boundaries

- Physical workout != source activity. Keep every recording. Count canonical parents once; children are explanatory, not additional totals.
- Garmin preferred where valid; Amazfit can supply missing physical evidence and elapsed timeline. Garmin physiology remains Garmin-specific.
- Running/source pace coverage is explicit; full reconstructed distance divided by incomplete Garmin timer is prohibited.
- Unknown != zero; confirmed training portions remain visible, whole unresolved amounts stay blank and candidate additions remain separate.
- GPS stays local; routine ZIP omits raw coordinates/tracks.
- User reports are authoritative context, but do not invent measured distance/weather from the reported October 6 example.
- No paid services, deployment, bridge API changes, sheet writes or plan changes. Cross-team interface communication uses the shared parent CONTEXT.md.

## Preprocessor Claude review — 2026-10-07

Reviewed ef58a28/7d3f687 against the checklist (source immutability, counting, provenance, pace denominators, unknown values, resolutions, determinism, ZIP privacy). Two identical reruns produce byte-identical canonical outputs; the routine ZIP has no FIT, record or coordinate data.

- **High, fixed: dual-watch runs double-counted when Garmin was paused right after starting.** Session starts then differ by more than the 300 s pair limit, so `match()` never pairs the watches and both became `direct` parents. Real cases: Aug 23 (18.34 km Garmin + 18.13 km Amazfit, 22.66 mi reported for one run) and Aug 27 (7.88 km + 7.87 km). Fix: `canonical_workouts.active_window()` trims short leading/trailing record blocks separated by >300 s gaps, and `build_groups()` joins otherwise-ungrouped Garmin/Amazfit singletons whose active windows meet the high-pair thresholds (start ≤120 s, overlap ≥90%, distance ≤10%). The parent is `duplicate_resolved` (Garmin primary) with an automatic `active_window_duplicate` issue recording the evidence. Source rows and `preprocess_fit.py` matching flags are unchanged. Aug 23 is now 11.397482 mi; Aug 27 4.895548 mi; week of Aug 17 22.34137 mi (was 33.608073). The four review items are unchanged. Tests: `test_early_garmin_pause_does_not_double_count_dual_watch_run`, `test_active_window_match_still_requires_distance_agreement`.
- **Medium, open (semantics): record-gap pauses can never auto-repair on Intervals exports.** These FITs carry no timer events, so every real Garmin pause is a `record_gap` labelled `device_dropout`, and the gap-repair path needs `timer_pause`.
